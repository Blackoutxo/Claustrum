# Full guide from first start

## First open
- Application opens with a interface with texts "Set your master key",

![img_3.png](setmk.png)

- You type in your password to be used,
- After entering the password, the application opens
- Application is in "Dashboard" where a button "+ Add Key" lies

![img_4.png](add_K.png)

**Caution:** Application only sets your first entered password AFTER you start saving your passwords otherwise it will continuously keep asking your for settings password. 

## Adding password to store
- Click "+ Add key" in "Dashboard"

![img_5.png](img_5.png)

- An interface opens
- Enter your title, Password & tags (You can press the little key thing button to generate a strong password automatically )
- Tags are like hash-tags it helps you narrow down to general items like (gmail, personal, game)
- After entering the details, click on save.
- Done! You saved your password.

## How to see your saved password details?

![img_6.png](card.png)

- After saving your passwords,
- In "Dashboard", There is small cards (box) with titles and coloured box with one letter inside it
- Click on the card,
- An interface opens
- Voila! All of your details is in the interface.

## How to favourite your password?

![img_7.png](favourite.png)

- As we have established about the passwords being a little cards,
- Every card has a Star icon on it rightest center,
- Press on the icon,
- It should be yellow,
- Voila! You have favourite-ed your password.
